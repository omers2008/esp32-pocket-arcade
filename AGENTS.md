# Pocket Arcade rules

- The ESP-NOW control entry (currently Floor Select) must always be the final
  entry at the bottom of the main game menu. Insert every new game before it.
- Keep game names, scored-game storage namespaces, and game dispatch aligned.
  Derive menu counts from the registration arrays; the ESP-NOW entry has no
  difficulty or high score.
- Preserve saved scores and joystick settings during firmware updates.
